LifeGuard AI

"Your Safety, Our Priority"

✓ 100% On-Device TensorFlow Lite AI • Zero Cloud AI APIs

LifeGuard AI is an advanced personal safety system featuring real-time ambient acoustic monitoring, threshold-activated deep sound analysis (~90 dB trigger), an interactive 10-second safety countdown, and automated GPS emergency dispatch with app-to-app alert networking.

DOWNLOAD ANDROID APK (v1.0.0) VIEW GITHUB SOURCE

🎙️ Continuous Sound Monitoring

Measures surrounding sound level locally in decibels (dB SPL). At approximately 90 dB or above, the system captures a short 3.0-second PCM audio buffer for deeper on-device intelligence.

🧠 On-Device Neural Network

Trained Mobile Audio CNN running directly inside Android via TensorFlow Lite (75.2 KB). Classifies sounds into Environmental, Human Normal, or Human Distress with zero cloud reliance.

⏳ 10-Second Safety Confirmation

Upon detecting potential vocal distress (≥70% confidence), launches the "Are You Safe?" screen. Users can cancel with "YES, I'M SAFE", or trigger emergency with "NO, HELP" / timeout.

📍 Automated Emergency SOS

Captures live GPS coordinates via Android Fused Location Provider, triggers audible alarms, and pushes instant incident telemetry to connected contacts through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

👥 Emergency Contacts Network

Direct app-to-app invitation and linking. When an incident activates, connected contacts receive an interactive alert displaying map coordinates, sound levels, and acknowledge buttons.

🌙 Automatic Night Safety Mode

Transitions into a high-visibility, dark safety theme after 6:00 PM, prioritizing SOS and emergency access without automatically forcing microphone activation.
